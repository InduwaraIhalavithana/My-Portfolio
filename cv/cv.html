Research

Blockchain and AI-Driven Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Framework for Secure Drug Traceability and Counterfeit Detection

Final-year researchGroup projectOngoingPoster presentation

M.R.T. Gimhan, P.I. Ihalavithana, N. Farook, E.M.U.W.J.B. Ekanayake · Uva Wellassa University

  • Problem: Sri Lanka's drug supply chain (NMRA → SPC → MSD → hospitals) runs on paper records that can be forged; in 2023 about 22,500 substandard immunoglobulin vials reached state hospitals.
  • Traceability: permissioned blockchain on Hyperledger Fabric with GS1 identifiers for tamper-proof, end-to-end tracking.
  • Detection: Isolation Forest and Autoencoder models flag suspicious and counterfeit transactions, with risk scores and real-time alerts stored on-chain.
  • Evaluation: accuracy, precision, recall and F1 for the models; throughput, latency and scalability with Hyperledger Caliper.
