Research

Blockchain and AI-Driven Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Framework for Secure Drug Traceability and Counterfeit Detection

Final-year researchGroup projectOngoingPoster presentation

M.R.T. Gimhan, P.I. Ihalavithana, N. Farook, E.M.U.W.J.B. Ekanayake · Uva Wellassa University

  • Problem. Sri Lanka's state drug supply chain (NMRA → SPC → MSD → hospitals) depends on paper records that can be forged; in 2023 about 22,500 substandard immunoglobulin vials reached state hospitals.
  • Traceability. A permissioned blockchain on Hyperledger Fabric with GS1 identifiers gives every pack a tamper-proof record across the whole chain.
  • Detection. Isolation Forest and Autoencoder models flag suspicious and counterfeit transactions, with risk scores and real-time alerts stored on-chain.
  • Evaluation. Models are measured on accuracy, precision, recall and F1; the network on throughput, latency and scalability with Hyperledger Caliper.
